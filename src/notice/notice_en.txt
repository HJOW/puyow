Sound playback is implemented, but the sound files
cannot be provided directly due to copyright issues.
Please refer to the following document.
https://github.com/HJOW/puyow/blob/main/docs/Sound.md

Once you have entered your AI service information on the
settings screen and the connection test succeeds, you can
choose "Solomon" as your opponent in battle modes such as
"Basic Rule" and "Fever Rule" to play against the AI.

However, commercial LLMs such as OpenAI take a long time
to respond, so the game is practically unplayable with them
for now. Please do not use them.

If you download the project, run the local Python server,
and connect to that local server to play the game,
you can choose "Local AI" in the AI service provider field
on the settings screen.

This Local AI has almost no delay, so you can choose
"Solomon" as your opponent and actually play the game.
